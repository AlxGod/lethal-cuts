LETHAL CUTS
===========

Lethal Cuts turns a long recording of a Commander game into short vertical clips
for YouTube Shorts and TikTok. You mark the good moments while the video plays,
drag a tall box over the part of the table to keep, and it makes ready-to-post
1080x1920 videos with the volume evened out.


INSTALLING
----------
1. Double-click "Lethal Cuts Setup 0.2.0.exe".
2. Windows will probably show a blue box: "Windows protected your PC".
   That's normal for programs made by a person rather than a big company.
   Click "More info", then "Run anyway".
3. Click through the installer. It puts a Lethal Cuts shortcut on your desktop.
   No admin password is needed.

To check everything works on your PC: Start menu > Lethal Cuts >
"Check that Lethal Cuts works". It plays and exports a tiny test video and
tells you if anything is wrong.


RECORDING A GAME (iPhone)
-------------------------
- Settings > Camera > Record Video > 4K at 30 fps.
- In the same menu, turn HDR Video OFF (HDR looks washed out after YouTube/TikTok).
- Clear space first: a 2-hour game is about 20 GB.
- Plug the phone in and turn on Do Not Disturb.
- Mount the phone over the table, looking down, and try not to bump it.
Copy the video to your PC (the .mov file works as it is, no converting).


USING IT
--------
1. Open the video (Open video button, or drag the file onto the window).
2. Skim with the 2x / 3x / 4x buttons or the arrow keys.
3. Press I when a great moment starts and O when it ends. The video keeps playing.
4. Click a clip's name in the list to name it (the name becomes the file name).
5. Drag the tall box on the video over the cards and players. Press P to preview
   exactly how the phone video will look.
6. Click "Export all clips". The finished videos go in a "<video name> Shorts"
   folder next to your video (you can change this in Settings).
7. Your finished clips wait in the Post tab (or click "Post clips"), from every
   game you've done. Click one to watch it, then "Post to YouTube" or "Post to
   TikTok": the upload page opens and the clip is highlighted in its folder, so
   you can drag it in. Its name is copied, so paste it as the title. Tick
   "Posted" once it's up and it moves to the Posted list, so you always know
   what's left. About one a day does better than posting them all at once.

Your work saves by itself in a small .lcproj file next to the video. Close the
program whenever you like and open the same video later to carry on.

The first time it opens, a short walkthrough shows you around. You can replay it
from the Guide tab, which also has recording tips, every hotkey, and tips for
strong Shorts. Every "?" button explains the control next to it.


UPDATING
--------
Run the new "Lethal Cuts Setup" over the old one. Your settings, saved clips and
Post list are kept.


UNINSTALLING
------------
Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Lethal Cuts > Uninstall.
(Your settings stay in %APPDATA%\Lethal Cuts and your .lcproj files stay next to
your videos, in case you install it again. Delete them by hand if you want them gone.)


CHANGING THE PROGRAM
--------------------
The "Lethal Cuts source" folder has the full source code. Open that folder in
Claude Code and describe the change you want; the CLAUDE.md file in it explains
how the program is organized and how to build a new installer.
